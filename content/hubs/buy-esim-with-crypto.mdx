Kokio checkout has two crypto options. Pay from your own Kokio Wallet in stablecoins, or from any wallet you already use through MoonPay. Either way, Kokio asks for no ID, name or email.

## Kokio Wallet: USDC, USDT or DAI on Base

The Kokio Wallet is a self-custodial smart wallet you create in the app with Face ID or your fingerprint. Setup takes a few minutes. Add USDC, USDT or DAI on Base, then pick **Kokio Wallet** at checkout and tap the token you want to pay with.

This route adds no payment fee. If you haven't set a wallet up yet, checkout offers to do it first.

## Any other wallet, through MoonPay

Pick **Other crypto wallet** at checkout to pay from a wallet on Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon or BNB Chain. MoonPay runs this payment and supports more tokens than the Kokio Wallet does. Its processing fee is added at checkout.

<HeadsUp>
MoonPay is a separate company with its own rules. Check what it asks for before you pay.
</HeadsUp>

## How to pay

1. Install Kokio and create your passkey.
2. Open **Shop**, pick a destination and a plan, then tap **View**.
3. Choose **Kokio Wallet** or **Other crypto wallet** and confirm the payment.
4. Once it confirms, tap **Install eSIM**.

Plan prices don't change with the payment method. A plan bought in crypto costs the same as one bought by card, before any processor fee.

One thing to know: payments from a Kokio Wallet are onchain, and onchain records are public and permanent. Nobody can delete them, including us.

Not paying in crypto? Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal and Link work too. [Browse destinations and prices](/esim).
